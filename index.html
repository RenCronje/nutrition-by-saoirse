Meet Saoirse

Your Teammate

Hi, I am Saoirse, a registered nutritionist but more importantly your partner in building a healthier happier life.

My Journey

My journey started in Cork, Ireland, where I earned my bachelor's degree in nutrition & health science and qualified as a Registered Nutritionist in 2022. But I realized quickly that true health isn't just about what you learn in a classroom; it's about life experience.

That realization took me to Thailand, where I worked in a detox resort helping people from all over the world transform their health and mindset. It was there I learned that nutrition isn't just fuel, it's a relationship. After a year abroad, I settled in Australia and founded Nutrition by Saoirse, turning a passion project into a global platform.

Today, I support clients worldwide - from FIFO and shift workers to busy parents - who are all striving to create sustainable lifestyles in a busy world.

I don't believe in handing you a cookie-cutter meal plan and walking away. My approach is realistic and education-based. I want to teach you how to understand your body so that nutrition fits into your life, not the other way around. I am here to coach you, support you, and share the knowledge I've gained to help you become the best version of yourself.

Core Pillars

I hold a bachelors degree in nutrition & health science. My coaching is built on clinical evidence and scientific understanding, not temporary social media trends.

I don't just tell you what to eat; I teach you how to fuel your body. My goal is to give you the knowledge—from understanding macros to gut health—so you can maintain your results for life without needing me forever.

We spend the first month nailing your sleep, meal prep, and protein intake before we ever look at cutting calories. A resilient metabolism is built on consistency and strong habits, not crash diets.

From my time in clinical detox centers to coaching FIFO workers, I know a "perfect plate" isn't always possible. I build flexible strategies that fit into busy schedules, shift work, and family chaos, ensuring the plan fits your life, not the other way around.

Whether you need a deep-dive weekly Zoom call or efficient voice-note feedback on the go, I offer tiered support structures designed to match your specific lifestyle and budget.

90 Day Foundation

My Method

From my journey to yours - The heart behind Nutrition by Saoirse.

You will learn how to fuel your body, boost your energy and live a healthier, happier life.

"Nailing the Basics" We start by hitting the reset button on your approach. For the first two weeks, I want you to forget about calorie deficits.
We can’t build a house without the foundations, your health is much the same, so we focus entirely on your habits first.

Weeks 1-2

We focus on sleep hygiene, consistent meal prep, and creating high-protein meals that actually satisfy you. We prioritize fiber and improving upon what you are currently eating, rather than restricting it.

Weeks 3-4

Once your habits are solid, we introduce the deficit. Because you are fueled and rested, this transition feels sustainable, not starving.

"Finding Your Flow" This is where the magic happens. In Month 2, we are nailing everything. The initial shock of change is gone, and you are now operating with a new level of consistency.

Month 2

  • Solidifying Meals: You've spent weeks learning what high-protein and high-fiber meals look like. You aren't guessing anymore. You know what fuels you and what that should look like.
  • Controlled Progress: We continue the deficit established in Week 3, but now it fits seamlessly into your life. This isn't about white-knuckling it; it's about proving to yourself that you can show up every single day.

"Mastering Your Body" Now that the weight is moving and the habits are set, we go deeper. This is what separates Nutrition by Saoirse from generic plans. We look at the internal health markers that keep you vibrant long-term.

Month 3

  • Gut Health & Diversity: We focus on your gut microbiome and increasing your "plant points" for immunity and digestion.
  • Intuitive Eating: We move away from strict tracking and start learning the "Hunger Scale". I teach you mindful eating practices so you can trust your body's signals again.

"Moving Your Plate from level 3 to level 10" I don't just tell you what to eat; I teach you why. My goal is to empower you so you never need a nutrition coach again.
Most plans hand you a rulebook; I teach you the game. Throughout our journey, we focus on specific educational pillars to ensure you are fueling effectively, not just cutting calories.

  • Mastering Macros: We move beyond "good vs. bad" food. You will learn how to balance protein, carbs, and fats to fuel your specific lifestyle and energy needs.
  • The Power of Micros: True health lives in the details. We focus on fiber, vitamins, and "eating the rainbow" to ensure you aren't just losing weight, but gaining vitality.
  • Mindset & Hunger Cues: Diet culture teaches you to ignore your body; I teach you to listen to it. We practice intuitive eating and using the hunger scale to build trust with your body again.
